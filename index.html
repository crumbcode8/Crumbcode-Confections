Our Menu

Seasonal flavors crafted with organic ingredients.

24 Carrot Gold

This spiced carrot cake is packed with crunchy walnuts and topped with a luscious, velvety swirl of cream cheese frosting. Delicate carrot shavings and a dash of cinnamon provide a charming finish to this cozy, garden-fresh favorite.

Orchid Heirloom

This comforting treat features a spiced apple cake with a hidden apple pie center, topped with a swirl of rich brown sugar buttercream. Finished with a generous dusting of golden crumble topping, it captures the nostalgic essence of a fresh-baked pie in every bite.

Gilded Cocoa Cloud

This rich chocolate cupcake is piled high with a decadent swirl of silky milk chocolate buttercream and finished with a delicate dusting of gold-toned sprinkles. Encased in a shimmering gold liner, it offers a sophisticated and deeply satisfying cocoa experience in every bite.

Blue Zest Bliss

This bright treat features a lemon blueberry cake filled with tangy lemon curd and topped with a smooth lemon cream cheese frosting. Finished with a sprinkle of fresh zest and a single plump blueberry, it offers a perfectly balanced burst of tart and sweet flavors.

The Main Squeeze

This vibrant treat features a zesty lemon cake hidden with a tart lemon curd center, all piled high with a swirl of intense raspberry buttercream. Crowned with a fresh berry, a lemon slice, and a whimsical straw, it’s a refreshing burst of sunshine in every bite.

Berry Modest

A fluffy vanilla bean cake conceals a heart of luscious strawberry reserves, topped with a silky swirl of classic vanilla buttercream. Garnished with a beautifully fanned fresh strawberry, this treat is a timeless, sweet-and-creamy delight that never goes out of style.

Midnight Orchid

This decadent chocolate cupcake hides a silky ganache core beneath a lush crown of velvety vanilla buttercream. Elegantly adorned with a delicate white flower and artistic chocolate flourishes, it is the perfect balance of deep cocoa and light, creamy sweetness.
